# Sales CRM – Multi-Vertical Sales Management & Performance Dashboard

## 1. Overview

The **Sales CRM** is a centralized sales management and performance-analysis platform designed to manage sales opportunities across multiple business-development verticals and channels.

The system provides the Sales Head and management team with complete visibility into:

- Where an opportunity originated
- Which BDE generated it
- Which BDM/Manager it was assigned to
- Client and requirement information
- Current sales stage
- Activities and follow-ups
- Vertical-specific sales funnel progression
- Conversion rates at different funnel stages
- Won and lost opportunities
- Revenue generated
- Revenue collected and pending
- Staffing requirements and candidate/profile movement
- Product demo performance
- Digital marketing service sales and payment status
- Individual opportunity history
- Team and vertical performance

The platform is designed around a **common CRM core with vertical-specific workflows**, because every sales channel follows a different acquisition and conversion process.

---

# 2. Business Objective

The primary objective is to provide the Sales Head with a single system to monitor the complete sales lifecycle.

The CRM should allow management to answer:

> Where are opportunities coming from?

> Who generated each opportunity?

> Who is responsible for converting it?

> What is the current status?

> What happened throughout the opportunity lifecycle?

> Which sales vertical is generating the most opportunities?

> Which vertical has the highest conversion rate?

> Where are opportunities dropping from the funnel?

> How much business has been won?

> How much revenue has actually been collected?

> Which BDEs are generating the most opportunities?

> Which BDMs are converting the most assigned opportunities?

# 5. Lead / Opportunity Management

Although the system is called a Sales CRM, the central business object is a **Lead / Opportunity**.

A Lead/Opportunity represents a potential business opportunity that needs to be tracked from acquisition through conversion or loss.

Each opportunity should have a unique identifier.

Example:

```text
UP-0001
LI-0001
EM-0001
CC-0001
ST-0001
DM-0001
PR-0001
```

The identifier can be generated automatically by the system.

# 8. Common Sales Pipeline

After opportunity creation, applicable sales opportunities move through a common sales progression:

```text
New Lead / Opportunity
        ↓
Client Response / Contacted
        ↓
Requirement Gathering
        ↓
Estimation / Proposal Shared
        ↓
Negotiation
        ↓
     ┌───────┴───────┐
     ↓               ↓
   WON              LOST
```

However, vertical-specific acquisition stages must also be tracked.

The common pipeline should therefore be considered the **shared sales execution layer**, not a replacement for each vertical's funnel.

# 13. Staffing Funnel

Staffing requires a different CRM relationship model.

A single client can have **multiple requirements**.

Therefore:

```text
Client
  ↓
Lead / Opportunity
  ↓
Multiple Requirements
  ↓
Candidates / Employee Profiles
  ↓
Profile Submission
  ↓
Client Shortlisting
  ↓
Interview
  ↓
Selection / Offer
  ↓
Placement / Won
```

The CRM should not create a completely separate client record every time the same client raises another requirement.

---

# 14. Staffing Client and Requirement Structure

Example:

```text
ABC Technologies
│
├── Requirement 1
│   └── Senior Java Developer – 3 Positions
│
├── Requirement 2
│   └── React Developer – 2 Positions
│
└── Requirement 3
    └── QA Engineer – 4 Positions
```

One client can have multiple active requirements simultaneously.

Each requirement should therefore have a unique Requirement ID.

Example:

```text
Client: ABC Technologies

REQ-001 – Senior Java Developer
REQ-002 – React Developer
REQ-003 – QA Engineer
```

# 15. Staffing Funnel – Exact Process

The staffing funnel is:

```text
Client Outreach
        ↓
Client Response
        ↓
Requirement Received
        ↓
Requirement Qualified
        ↓
Employee / Candidate Sourcing
        ↓
Profiles Shared Against Requirement
        ↓
Client Shortlisted Profiles
        ↓
Interview
        ↓
Selection / Offer
        ↓
Placement / Won
        ↓
Lost / Not Selected
```

## 15.1 Client Outreach

The staffing team reaches potential clients.

## 15.2 Client Response

The client responds.

## 15.3 Requirement Received

The client provides one or more staffing requirements.

## 15.4 Requirement Qualified

The requirement is reviewed and confirmed.

Information can include:

- Position
- Number of openings
- Required skills
- Experience
- Location
- Budget
- Timeline
- Other requirement details

## 15.5 Employee / Candidate Sourcing

Potential employees/candidates are identified.

## 15.6 Profiles Shared Against Requirement

Candidate/employee profiles are shared specifically against the requirement.

This is a critical staffing stage and must be explicitly tracked.

## 15.7 Client Shortlisted Profiles

The client reviews and shortlists submitted profiles.

## 15.8 Interview

Shortlisted candidates enter the interview process.

## 15.9 Selection / Offer

Candidate is selected and moves toward an offer.

## 15.10 Placement / Won

The placement is completed.

## 15.11 Lost / Not Selected

Requirement or candidate does not convert.

# 37. Key Design Principles

### Centralized CRM

All opportunities should be accessible from one platform.

### Vertical-Specific Funnels

Different sales verticals must retain their actual business process rather than being forced into one generic funnel.

### Ownership Visibility

The system must clearly distinguish:

**Generated By → Assigned To**

### Complete Opportunity History

Management should be able to see the complete lifecycle.

### Staffing Relationship Model

One client can have multiple requirements, and each requirement can have multiple candidates/profiles.

### Commercial Visibility

Won business and collected revenue should be tracked separately.

### Conversion Measurement

Management should be able to measure both overall and stage-to-stage conversion.

### Drill-Down Reporting

Dashboard metrics should be clickable so the Sales Head can navigate from:

**KPI → Vertical → Opportunity List → Individual Opportunity → Full Activity History**

# 39. Expected Business Outcome

The CRM should provide management with a single source of truth for sales operations.

It should enable the organization to:

- Capture opportunities consistently.
- Track where opportunities originate.
- Track BDE lead-generation performance.
- Track BDM conversion performance.
- Monitor vertical-specific sales funnels.
- Identify bottlenecks in the sales process.
- Monitor open pipeline.
- Improve conversion rates.
- Track staffing requirements and placements.
- Track product demos and orders.
- Track digital marketing contracts and payments.
- Understand revenue generated versus revenue collected.
- Identify lost opportunities and reasons.
- Make data-driven sales decisions.

# 40. Success Criteria

The project can be considered successful when the Sales Head can perform the following without needing separate spreadsheets or manual consolidation:

1. View all sales opportunities in one dashboard.
2. Filter opportunities by vertical/source.
3. Identify who generated an opportunity.
4. Identify who is responsible for the opportunity.
5. View the complete opportunity history.
6. Track the appropriate funnel for each sales vertical.
7. View conversion rates at individual funnel stages.
8. Compare BDE and BDM performance.
9. Manage multiple staffing requirements under one client.

# 41. Final Product Summary

The Sales CRM is a **multi-vertical sales management, pipeline tracking, conversion analytics, and revenue visibility platform**.

Its architecture should follow the principle:

```text
Different Sales Sources
          ↓
Centralized CRM
          ↓
Common Client / Opportunity Data
          ↓
Vertical-Specific Sales Funnels
          ↓
Individual Opportunity Tracking
          ↓
Conversion & Revenue Analytics
          ↓
Sales Head Dashboard
```

The CRM is therefore not simply a database of leads. It is a **sales-management system that connects lead generation, ownership, pipeline progression, vertical-specific processes, conversion performance, revenue realization, and management reporting in one platform.**
